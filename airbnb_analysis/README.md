# Airbnb Market Analysis

- Hosts with 10 or more listings own 37.1% of the listings in Columbus but only 17.2% in the Twin Cities, so Columbus has more big hosts
- The top 10% of listings in Chicago make 40.7% of the city's estimated revenue, so a small group of listings is bringing in a lot of the money.
- North Linden is the cheapest option that is solid in Columbus for a family of four, at about $32.60 per person a night.
- Twin Cities reviews were highest in August 2025, about 2.37 times more than the slowest month, so summer is way busier there.
- Superhosts in Chicago charge about 44 more a night, but in the Twin Cities they actually charge about $1 less, so being a Superhost doesn't always mean you can charge more.
